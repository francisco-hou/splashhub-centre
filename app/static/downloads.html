How to install an extension from a .zip
  1. Download it and unzip it to a folder you’ll keep (Chrome loads it from there).
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Press Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. To update later: download the new version, unzip it over the same folder, then press the reload icon on the extension’s card.
